M-VAVE FM-1 EMULATOR (fm1-live) — ReMOTE 61 SL TEMPLATE
Template file: M-VAVE FM-1.syx
Emulator change: fm1-live-midi-panel.patch (adds MIDI control of the FM-1's panel)

The emulator normally sends all MIDI into the FM-1's MIDI IN jack, which plays the firmware.
The patch adds a panel channel: control changes on MIDI channel 16 now turn the FM-1's
knobs and press its buttons, as the mouse does. Your keys still play the synth on their channel.

WHAT EACH CONTROL DOES  (all on MIDI channel 16)
  Encoder 1 ............ MASTER        CC 20   relative
  Encoder 2 ............ SELECT        CC 21   relative
  Encoder 3 ............ PRESETS       CC 22   relative
  Encoder 4 ............ ALGORITHM     CC 23   relative
  Encoders 5–8 ......... KNOB1–KNOB4   CC 24–27 relative
  Right upper 1–6 ...... FX SCL ENV LFO EDIT GLO     CC 104–109
  Right lower 1–6 ...... HOME SAVE ARP SEQ PLAY REC  CC 110–115
  Left lower 1–2 ....... OCT- / OCT+                 CC 102–103
  Pedals, mod wheel, pitch bend: as usual, on the keyboard's channel

  The SL's own Octave buttons are fixed hardware functions a template can't reassign, so
  OCT- and OCT+ are on Left Lower buttons 1 and 2. The SL's octave buttons still transpose
  the notes you play into the emulator.

ONE-TIME SETUP: ADD PANEL CONTROL TO THE EMULATOR
In Terminal, in your fm1-emu folder (or clone it first:
  git clone https://github.com/joakimeriksson/fm1-emu.git && cd fm1-emu):

  git apply "/path/to/fm1-live-midi-panel.patch"
  cargo run --release -p fm1-live -- --fw felucca

When it starts it prints "MIDI panel control on channel 16 …". Options:
  --panel-ch N   use another channel for the panel (0 turns panel control off)
  --midi-ch 2    for M-VAVE's stock firmware, which listens to notes on channel 2;
                 this moves your keys to channel 2 and leaves the panel on channel 16

ON THE KEYBOARD
Open "M-VAVE FM-1.syx" in SL Template Editor, press Send This Template, then Write on the
keyboard to keep it in a slot.
